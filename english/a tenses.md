# Simple

## Present Simple
**Use:** Facts, habits, routines, universal truths.
**Urdu synonym:** تا ہوں, تا ہے, تی ہے۔, تے ہیں۔ etc
**Helping verbs:** Do, Does, Am, Is, Are
**Examples:**
- I drink coffee every morning. (habit)  
- She works in a hospital. (fact)  
- The sun rises in the east. (universal truth)

## Past Simple
**Use:** Completed actions in the past.
**Urdu synonym:** تا تھا، تی تھی، تے تھے , سیکھی, کی, گيا, گئ, گئۓ, يا  etc
**Helping verbs:** Did, Was, Were
**Examples:**  
- I walked to school yesterday.  
- She ate lunch.  
- They played football.

## Future Simple
**Use:** Future actions and predictions.
**Urdu synonym:** گا, گی, گے etc
**Helping verbs:** Will, Shall
**Examples:**  
- I will walk to school.
- She will eat lunch.
- They will play football.
